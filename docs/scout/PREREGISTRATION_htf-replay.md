# Pre-registration: htf-trend historical replay

Registered 2026-10-24 before this replay implementation produced any result. This study is a historical **prior** for the forward paper test. It cannot pass, fail, graduate or kill the lane. The paper record from 2026-08-06 remains the only out-of-sample gate.

## Frozen design

- Universe: all Hyperliquid mainnet perps available at each historical bar, including later delisted perps. A dated listing manifest records each coin's first and last tradable day. HL's current `meta.universe` is only a current-listing proxy and cannot recover delisted assets or listing dates. A proxy run must say `incomplete universe` and cannot stand in for the full replay. First and last candle dates may bound availability, but candle coverage alone does not prove listing dates. Report the registered BTC, ETH, SOL and HYPE subset separately, using each coin only after its own listing.
- Rule: use the live lane's completed daily close beyond the prior 20 closes. Set the hard stop to `min(2 × ATR20 / signal close, 0.12)`. Exit on a completed close through the opposite prior 10-close channel, or a hard stop. There is no target or pyramiding.
- Timing: the signal from day `t` can first execute at day `t+1` open. The same lag applies to the 10-day exit. An open stop is active from entry. If the next bar opens beyond the stop, fill at that open plus adverse execution costs. In a bar with a stop touch, take the stop before any later close signal. Missing daily bars cannot create a trade.
- Execution: use the next daily open, plus 16 basis points of adverse overshoot on each entry and exit, and 4.5 basis points of taker fee per side. The 16 basis points are the desk's measured mean for price-triggered opens and are an explicit conservative proxy for exits. Express net P&L in R using the planned stop distance from the executable entry. Include each historical hourly funding payment during the holding period, with positive funding paid by longs and received by shorts. Reject missing funding coverage rather than treating it as zero.
- Portfolio: process all eligible coins on one shared clock. Permit one htf-trend position per coin and at most two concurrent same-direction positions among BTC, ETH, SOL and HYPE. Use a fixed alphabetical tie break for simultaneous signals. Report blocked entries. Do not independently sum uncapped coin results.
- Folds: use non-overlapping calendar-year entry cohorts and report each year's mean net R and count. Keep cross-boundary holds with their entry cohort. Do not optimise parameters on these folds.
- Uncertainty: assign entries to non-overlapping 5-day calendar episodes. Resample whole episode blocks with replacement, preserving all trades in each episode. Report portfolio mean net R, a seeded 95% percentile confidence interval, and `P(edge > 0)` from that bootstrap. Also report counts of trades, episodes, years, missing coverage and delisted assets. A confidence interval that lies entirely below zero would prompt a recommendation for the scheduled retro to consider stopping the lane. It would not change the forward gate automatically.

## Forward-bar calibration

Simulate samples of 20 closes from whole 5-day entry episodes. Report the probability under a zero-edge distribution and under the replay's estimated distribution that **all** registered conditions hold: raw mean at least +0.25R, mean times 0.7 positive, and the raw +0.25R threshold still met after removing the largest 5-day entry cluster. The zero-edge distribution centres episode returns at zero while retaining dispersion and within-episode dependence. Report Monte Carlo counts and seed. These probabilities describe the registered checkpoint's power and false-pass rate. They do not revise its bar.

Historical data can omit delisted contracts, early candles, funding payments or actual order-book fills. The report must mark any such coverage gap and avoid a complete-universe claim. No historical finding changes live firing.
